K-Means starts by placing k random cluster centers, then repeats two steps until stable: (1) assign every point to its nearest center, and (2) move each center to the average position of the points assigned to it. There are no labels anywhere in this process — the algorithm only looks at how close points are to each other, which is what makes it unsupervised.
